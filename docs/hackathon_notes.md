# Hackathon Notes

## 1. Key Takeaways from Gary’s Video

### The Problem: Weeds and Agricultural Pests
- Weeds and pests are becoming increasingly difficult to manage.
- Chemical solutions struggle to keep up and may not provide an effective long-term answer.

### The Role of AI
- AI systems can identify individual weeds and pests in real time.
- These systems may combine:
  - Sensors
  - Computer vision
  - Robotics
  - Automated intervention systems

### AI and Bioengineering
- Bioengineering approaches also show promise, including:
  - RNA-based solutions
  - Other biotechnology techniques
- AI is not limited to one specific intervention. It can augment and accelerate scientific breakthroughs across agriculture, biology, robotics, and related fields.

## 3. Project Requirements

The project should:

- Relate to a **YC Request for Startups (RFS)**.
- Be substantially built during the hackathon.
- Be developed by participants at the event.
- Be complete enough to submit and demonstrate.
- Include all required submission materials.

Only complete submissions will be considered.

---

## 4. Required Submission Information

Each submission should include:

1. **Project name**
2. **Team name**
3. **Team member names**
4. **Team member email addresses**
5. **Product description**
6. **YC RFS being targeted**
7. **GitHub repository URL**
8. **Video, demo, or pitch URL**
   - The video/demo/pitch link should also be included in the GitHub repository.

## 5. Judging Perspective

Teams should evaluate their submission from the perspective of a YC partner:

> How would a YC partner assess this project, video, submission, and amount of progress given the limited time available?

### Questions to Consider
- Is the problem important and clearly defined?
- Is the proposed solution compelling?
- Did the team make meaningful progress during the event?
- Is there a working demo?
- Does the project target a real user need?
- Does the team demonstrate speed, resourcefulness, and technical execution?
- Is the product connected clearly to a YC RFS?
- Could the project become a real startup?
