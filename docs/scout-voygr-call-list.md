## Call script for the VOYGR agent

Keep it under 45 seconds before the first question. Voice agents lose people in the preamble.

> "Hi, I'm an AI assistant calling on behalf of Scout, a student hackathon project — I'll be quick and you can hang up any time. We're building software that lets a spray or scouting drone reason during the flight: it spots a disease symptom, drops altitude to confirm, checks the neighbouring row, and lands with a bounded treatment zone instead of a folder of images. I have three questions if you have two minutes."

1. When one of your pilots sees something off in a block, how many separate trips does it take before anything gets sprayed?
2. What do you charge per hectare, and has that number moved in the last two years?
3. If a flight came back with a bounded treatment zone plus the evidence behind it, could you charge more for the same flight — and what would you pay for that?

Close: **"Can I send you a two-minute demo? And if it's useful, would you run a paid pilot this season?"**

**Log per call:** business, country, local time, whether a human answered, the multi-trip answer verbatim, the per-hectare number, the willingness-to-pay number, and any yes-to-pilot. One verbatim quote plus one price point is a traction slide. A paid pilot is the Hexclave prize.
